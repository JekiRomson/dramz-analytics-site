Dramz Analytics

Internal analytics tool of Dramz

Dramz Analytics is an internal, non-public tool used by the Dramz team to analyze the performance of Dramz’s own social media channels (YouTube first; Instagram and TikTok later).

With the permission of the channel owner, the tool reads channel and video statistics through the official YouTube API Services — read-only. It never uploads, edits, deletes or publishes anything on the channel. The collected statistics are shown to the Dramz team in an internal dashboard.

The tool is not offered to the public and has no user sign-up.

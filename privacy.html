Privacy Policy

Dramz Analytics · Effective date: October 18, 2026

Dramz Analytics (“the tool”) is an internal analytics tool of Dramz. It is used only by the Dramz team to analyze Dramz’s own social media channels. This policy explains what data the tool accesses and how it is handled.

1. Who grants access

Access is granted only by the owner of a Dramz channel, once, through Google’s standard consent screen. The tool does not offer accounts or sign-up to the public.

2. What data the tool accesses

Through the YouTube API Services, with read-only permissions (youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly), the tool reads:

  • channel and video information: titles, publication dates, durations;
  • video and channel statistics: views, watch time, likes, comments count, shares, subscribers gained and lost, impressions and click-through rate;
  • aggregated audience reports provided by YouTube: countries, age groups and gender (as percentages), traffic sources, device types.

Audience reports are aggregated by YouTube; they do not identify individual viewers. Public comments on Dramz’s own videos (comment text and the commenter’s public display name, as shown on YouTube) may be processed for content analysis.

The tool cannot upload, edit, delete or publish anything on the channel and does not access the owner’s password, email or other Google account data.

3. How the data is used

Solely to show the performance of Dramz’s channels to the Dramz team in an internal dashboard and to analyze content. The data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with third parties.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored in a private database on a server in the European Union, closed to public access. The access token issued by Google is stored only on that server, protected by file permissions, and is never shown or transmitted elsewhere. Database backups are encrypted in transit and stored with restricted access.

5. Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept for as long as they are needed for analysis of Dramz’s channels. Channel and video information obtained from the YouTube API Services is refreshed regularly. Data is deleted on request of the channel owner (see contact below).

6. Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the tool’s access at any time in their Google Account: myaccount.google.com/permissions → “Dramz Analytics” → remove access. After that, the tool can no longer read any data.

7. YouTube and Google

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By granting access, the channel owner is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. For how Google handles data, see the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date.
